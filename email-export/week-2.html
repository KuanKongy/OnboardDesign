What changed this week
Updated Apply for BC MSP health insurance. Now urgent. The wait clock does not start until you apply, and the application is 20 minutes online.
Updated Apply for a Social Insurance Number (SIN). The campus clinic moved to the Life Building and now runs every Tuesday.
New Get a BC Services Card or BCID (photo ID). No rush — within your first 2 months. Once your MSP application is in, you can get local photo ID and leave your passport at home.
Off the list Get a Compass Card and link your U-Pass. Most of you are tapping in already, and your U-Pass now renews monthly on its own.
Off the list Double-check your study permit. If you checked it and it was fine, you're done. If you found an error, book International Student Advising now instead of waiting.
'Off the list' just means we stop reminding you — anything you haven't finished stays in your tracker.
