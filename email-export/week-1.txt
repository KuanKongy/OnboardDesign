Subject: Your first week at UBC: 4 things to do now

UBC Arrival Guide — Issue #1 · Monday, Aug 31, 2026

Welcome to Vancouver! There's a lot of noise in your inbox right now, so here is the only list you need this week. Four tasks unlock almost everything else — do them in this order. Everything below comes straight from your Arrival Tracker, which always has the current version with full steps and peer notes.

DO THESE NOW — IN THIS ORDER
1. Double-check your study permit (First 1–2 days · ~10 min)
   Make sure the permit you received at the border is correct — errors are much easier to fix now.
2. Get a Compass Card and link your U-Pass (First 3 days · ~30 min + travel)
   Your transit card for buses, SkyTrain and SeaBus — your student U-Pass loads onto it.
3. Set up a Canadian phone number (First 3 days · ~45 min)
   A local number is needed for banking, your SIN, deliveries, and almost every sign-up form.
4. Apply for a Social Insurance Number (SIN) (First 2 weeks · ~1 hour + travel)
   You need a SIN before you can be paid for any job in Canada — it is free and takes one visit.

COMING UP
- Confirm your iMED temporary health coverage (First 2 weeks)
- Open a Canadian bank account (First 2–3 weeks)
- Apply for BC MSP health insurance (Apply in month 1)
