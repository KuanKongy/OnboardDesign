Subject: Your first week at UBC: 5 things, in order

UBC Arrival Guide — Issue #1 · Monday, Aug 31, 2026

Welcome to Vancouver. Your inbox is probably chaos right now, so here's the short list: five things this week, in the order that saves you repeat trips. The permit check goes first because your SIN application and your bank account both need a correct permit. Moving faster than one week? Go ahead — the full list is already in your tracker.

DO THIS WEEK — IN THIS ORDER
1. Double-check your study permit (First 1–2 days · ~10 min)
   Five minutes now saves weeks later — your SIN application and your bank account both need a correct permit.
2. Apply for a Social Insurance Number (SIN) (First 2 weeks · ~1 hour + travel)
   You need a SIN before you can be paid for any job in Canada — it is free and takes one visit.
3. Open a Canadian bank account (First 2 weeks · ~1 hour)
   A no-fee student account lets you pay rent, get paid, and stop paying foreign card fees — and phone plans are cheaper with one.
4. Set up a Canadian phone number (First few days · ~45 min)
   A local number is needed for banking, your SIN, deliveries, and almost every sign-up form.
5. Get a Compass Card and link your U-Pass (First 3 days · ~30 min + travel)
   Your transit card for buses, SkyTrain and SeaBus — your student U-Pass loads onto it.
